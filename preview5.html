4. Why They Might Buy

Finance leadership disruption

Three senior finance changes in ~90 days (CFO, Controller, CAE adjustments) create an urgent need for consistent, auditable financial processes. ERP reduces spreadsheet dependency, centralizes ledgers, and speeds month-end close — lowering operational risk during transitions.

Efficiency and cash discipline mandate

CEO directive to prioritise "resiliency over growth" means tighter CAPEX and focus on cash conversion. ERP enables multi-entity cash visibility, scenario planning, and automated intercompany eliminations — critical to protect margins under constrained CAPEX.

Outdated HCM across 64K employees

Ramco (2014) is aging: payroll complexity, compliance across jurisdictions, and talent mobility are pain points. A modern ERP HCM module reduces manual HR work, improves compliance, and supports workforce planning tied to cost control goals.

JV and investor governance expectations

Mitsubishi's investment raises the bar for governance and reporting. ERP helps meet international audit and reporting standards, important for JV partners and future capital raises.
